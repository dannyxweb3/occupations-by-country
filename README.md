# AI Job Risk Career Guides

An open bilingual knowledge base of career and occupation guides across 46 countries.

Explore the published career intelligence platform at
[AIJobRisk.com](https://aijobrisk.com/).

## What Each Guide Covers

Depending on country and occupation, guides may include:

- Education pathways, duration, and cost
- Qualifications and licensing requirements
- Entry, mid-career, and senior salary ranges
- Employment outlook and job demand
- AI exposure and automatable job tasks
- AI-enhanced tasks and human advantages
- Career transition opportunities
- Skilled migration and work visa pathways
- Data sources and frequently asked questions

## Data and Methodology

The guides combine occupational classifications, national statistical
sources, labour-market publications, salary data, qualification rules,
migration information, and structured AI-exposure assessments.

Sources and definitions vary by country. Salary, migration, licensing,
and outlook information may change over time. Always verify important
decisions with the linked official source.

Read the current methodology at
[AIJobRisk.com/methodology](https://aijobrisk.com/methodology).

## Languages

English and Chinese documents are language variants of the same
occupation-country record. They are maintained under separate language
paths and may be updated at different times.
